About this scene

A model of the southern Mariana Trench, down to the deepest point in the GMRT seafloor grid, 10,932 m below sea level. Heights are drawn 6 times taller than they are, so the trench reads in 3D. Play runs a dive to the deepest point, cuts a slice through it and sets Everest into the slice at the same scale: its peak stays 2,083 m under the surface. A click on the model reads the depth from the same grid, averaged to cells of about 480 m so the browser can load it.
