About this scene

Mainland Norway as a model, with every hydropower plant in the WRI Global Power Plant Database and every 300 and 420 kV power line in OpenStreetMap drawn onto it. Plants are sized by their capacity; the database lists only the large plants and dates from 2021, so small hydro and most wind farms are missing. Heights are drawn 9 times taller than they are.
