The rain that drowned Florence

About this map

The basin is every HydroBASINS level 12 unit that drains through the unit holding Ponte Vecchio. The rivers come from HydroRIVERS, followed through their NEXT_DOWN links. The area is measured in EPSG:3035 and the length of the Arno in EPSG:32632. Relief and contours come from the Copernicus DEM. Each reach's distance to Florence is its distance along the network, not in a straight line.
