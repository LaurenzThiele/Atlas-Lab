About this map

Each road's distance from Rome is the shortest way there along the network. Road lengths are measured on the WGS84 ellipsoid.

Parts of the network with no road link to Rome, such as Britain, are tied in by one straight sea crossing each. A crossing counts toward the distance on the timeline, but it is never drawn and is in no total.
